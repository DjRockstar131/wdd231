World & How to Use This Bestiary

The Setting

The Arcane Bestiary imagines a world where magic surges beneath the earth like buried lightning. Ancient dragons ride stormfronts, dead kings whisper from flooded crypts, and ordinary beasts twist into strange new forms after exposure to wild magic.

You can drop these creatures into any D&D setting by reskinning names and locations, or treat them as denizens of your own homebrew world.

Reading the Stat Blocks

  • CR (Challenge Rating): A rough indicator of how tough the creature is.
  • Type: Broad creature category, such as Beast, Undead, or Dragon.
  • Environment: Common locations where the creature is found.
  • Hooks & Abilities: Short lore snippets and mechanical ideas to inspire encounters.

Tuning for Your Table

If a creature looks a little too strong or too weak, adjust its hit points, damage, or number of uses per day. The important part is the idea: a storm-chasing drake, a graveyard choir of wailing spirits, or a stag crowned with crystal antlers.

You are encouraged to bend, break, and remix these monsters so they fit the tone and power level of your own campaign.
