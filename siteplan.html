Site Plan: Arcane Bestiary

A Dungeons & Dragons inspired creature compendium.

Subject: A Dungeons and Dragons Bestiary

Site Name

Arcane Bestiary

This name was chosen because it clearly communicates that the site is a magical, fantasy-themed collection of creatures. The word Arcane reflects spellcasting and mystery in Dungeons & Dragons, while Bestiary is a classic term for a catalog of monsters and beasts.

Example domain (for class purposes): arcanebestiary.net

Site Purpose

The website will present a bestiary of fantasy creatures in Dungeons & Dragons. The site will include:

  • A home page that introduces the bestiary, explains its theme and how it can support D&D players and Dungeon Masters.
  • A full creature index that displays at least fifteen monsters, dynamically generated from a JSON data file. Visitors will be able to browse, filter, and view key stats and lore.
  • An informational page about the world and how to use the site, including an explanation of monster attributes (CR, type, environment) and how to plug the creatures into encounters.

Overall, the purpose is to give players a quickly browsable, flavorful resource of homebrew-style monsters that can be dropped into campaigns with minimal prep.

Scenarios

The following example questions represent common scenarios for the target audience:

  • “I’m running a session in a haunted forest. Which creatures fit that environment and CR range?”
    The bestiary index will allow visitors to filter monsters by environment (such as forest, swamp, dungeon) and by challenge rating so they can quickly find appropriate options.
  • “I need a unique boss monster with lore that ties into my campaign’s story.”
    Each creature entry will include a short lore section plus hooks or abilities that make it easy to connect the monster to existing plots, factions, or locations.
  • “What do all these stats mean and how do I use them at the table?”
    The informational page will include a gentle explanation of D&D terms like CR, type, and environments, along with tips for scaling and reskinning creatures.

Color Scheme

The color scheme is inspired by dimly lit dungeons and spellfire. At least two main colors will be used:

  • Dark Arcane Night – #050816
    Used for the main background of the site and panels, creating a high-contrast, moody base that feels like a starless sky over an adventuring party.
  • Fiery Glyph Orange – #f97316
    Used for key accents: primary buttons, important headings, and hover states. This gives the feel of burning runes or torchlight illuminating a monster’s lair.
  • Emerald Sigil Green – #22c55e
    Used as a secondary accent for section labels, small highlights, and tag badges (for example, “Forest” or “Undead”). It calls to mind magical wards and healing spells.

Typography

The project will use two main fonts:

Primary Heading Font

Cinzel – Headings, Logo, and Decorative Titles

Usage: Applied to the site name in the header, main page titles (H1), section headings (H2 / H3), and any fantasy-styled labels such as “Bestiary Index” or “Encounter Seeds”.

Body Font

Inter – Body Text, UI, and Descriptions

Usage: Applied to general body copy, navigation links, card descriptions, and form labels. Inter is chosen for readability on both mobile and desktop while still fitting a modern fantasy interface.

Additional stylistic emphasis (for example, creature names in small caps or italicized lore snippets) will be handled using these same fonts with CSS variations rather than extra typefaces.
